# 🔬 Mindset Builder Agent - Research & Findings

## 1. Domain Mindset vs Skill Acquisition
- **The Core Thesis:** Skills are algorithmic, task-oriented, and increasingly automated by AI. Mindset is epistemological—it defines how an expert perceives incomplete information, forms hypotheses, navigates risk, and arrives at decisions.
- **Cognitive Mapping via Analogies:** Humans assimilate abstract, complex systems fastest when anchored to familiar, visceral physical paradigms (e.g., QA = Preventive Medicine / Air Traffic Control / Popperian Falsification).
- **The Tri-Tier Analogy Spectrum:**
  1. **Easy (Visceral / Daily Life):** Intuitive, immediately relatable by non-experts (e.g. Doctor & Patient, Master Chef & Kitchen Line).
  2. **Medium (Systemic / Operational):** Explains orchestration, multi-variable interactions, and structural workflows (e.g. Airport Control Tower, Forensic Crime Scene Investigator).
  3. **Deep / Advanced (Philosophical / First-Principles):** Explores epistemology, game theory, chaos dynamics, or falsification (e.g. Karl Popper's Falsificationism, Military OODA Loop, Immune System Homeostasis).
