# 🎓 SOP 03: Chapter-Wise Mindset Induction & Socratic Drills

## 1. Purpose
Transform theoretical analogies into permanent cognitive reflexes through a 5-chapter pedagogical curriculum featuring interactive scenario drills.

## 2. The 5-Chapter Progression
1. **Chapter 1: The Perceptual Lens (Epistemology)**
   - How an expert in this field perceives the system compared to a novice.
   - The primary filter: what noise they filter out and what subtle signals they notice.
2. **Chapter 2: The Diagnostic Framework (Triage & Isolation)**
   - How to deconstruct ambiguity and trace symptoms back to root causes using the chosen analogy.
3. **Chapter 3: The Heuristics & Decision Calculus**
   - High-stakes trade-offs under incomplete information (Speed vs. Rigor, False Positives vs. False Negatives).
4. **Chapter 4: The Crucible (Interactive Scenario Drill)**
   - A realistic, high-pressure dilemma.
   - The user must choose an action and is critiqued on whether their decision reflects a "procedural task-follower" vs. "master mindset practitioner".
5. **Chapter 5: Mindset Synthesis & Daily Rituals**
   - The internal axioms, daily habits, and reflective practices that keep the mindset sharp and impervious to AI commoditization.

## 3. Feedback Loop
When evaluating scenario drills, provide immediate, constructive feedback:
- Affirm what cognitive instinct was triggered.
- Show what blind spot was missed.
- Reinforce the anchor analogy.
